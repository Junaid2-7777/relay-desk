# Relay Desk roadmap

**The first release should bring one decision back to the household.** In a single session, book an eligible service visit, keep watching a promised refund, and ask about the bill that exceeds the person's mandate. A judge should be able to change all three outcomes and introduce an unfamiliar fourth job.

## What earns a release claim

- **A general desk** needs all seven deterministic checks. After the build is frozen, an evaluator supplies an unfamiliar goal through normal intake. Test both tracking a new outcome and taking an authorized action for a different issuer through an existing tool. The old issuer’s grant cannot authorize the new action. Where no appropriate observer exists, the desk must identify the missing evidence and keep the duty open.
- **A trusted actor** needs zero unauthorized or duplicate effects, false completions, missed mandatory approvals or sensitive-data leaks. A one-off ₹1,180 approval can never raise tomorrow's ₹900 cap. Reserve the live-model claim for at least 33 of 36 successful held-out trials, including all 12 blocked cases, under the full evaluation rules.
- **Work taken off the household** needs successful outcomes alongside fewer avoidable decisions, interruptions or lookups than the matched manual baseline. Run once with no permissions and once with established grants. Include consent, refusals and later clarifications when measuring effort across the whole delegation. Judge model value separately against the fixed-rule desk. Use observed participant results for claims about time or willingness to delegate. [Evaluation and comparison protocol](docs/evaluation.md)

If time slips, cut live fetching, extra specialties, deployment and additional screens first. Keep the shared controller, the three simultaneous duties, a judge-defined fourth task, explicit consent, independently readable receipts and at least one failed-provider recovery. If live inference remains unavailable, demonstrate the fixture-driven local writes and publish the live-model gate as pending.

## From a working demo to a useful service

Test with adults who already organize appointments, bills or returns. Ask each to reconstruct one recent real errand before showing the synthetic brief. Identify which actions they would delegate, what would make them revoke permission, and whether the desk returned the right decisions. Record refusals and confusion alongside successful walkthroughs. [Research protocol](docs/problem.md)

Before a pilot, have one willing participant and provider establish a permitted way to read the relevant confirmation, its stable reference and its freshness. A bank credit may omit the merchant reference. A service company may offer no verifiable booking feed. In either case, the desk can prepare work, show the gap and request review. Expansion depends on a reliable way to prove that particular outcome. Start with read-only follow-up and reversible calendar work. Evaluate money-moving capabilities separately before inviting a household to authorize them.
