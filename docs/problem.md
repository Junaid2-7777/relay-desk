# The work a household can hand over

**“Get my refund back, book the broadband visit around my calendar, and take care of the electricity bill within my limit.”** One person often has to remember all three jobs long after the calls and forms are over. They want to leave for work knowing what someone else will handle and what still needs their judgement.

Relay Desk is a proposed household chief of staff for that person. Each delegation gives it an outcome to pursue, specific permission to act, and a way to tell when the job is finished. It can carry an open commitment across a quiet day, compare new evidence with the owner’s instruction, do the permitted work and bring back a decision when the instruction runs out.

## A Monday with the desk

On **Monday 23 November 2026, India Standard Time**, three unrelated records arrive for the same household. Every amount, account, communication and calendar entry in this example is synthetic.

| What the owner handed over | What the desk should do | When the owner hears from it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Recover ₹1,249 for a return.** The merchant says it has issued the refund. | Keep the refund open and check the authorized settlement feed for an incoming credit matching this return, account, currency and amount. Keep the merchant’s promise with the file. | A pending refund can wait quietly until its agreed checkpoint. A missed promise may warrant one specific follow-up for review. Show a completed refund only after the matching credit posts. |
| **Book one free broadband visit on Tuesday afternoon.** Existing household commitments are protected. | The provider offers 11 am and 3 pm on 24 November. The earlier slot overlaps an existing event and falls outside the grant. Recheck the free 3 pm slot, book it with the provider, then enter the confirmed visit in the calendar. | The booking goes into the brief with the confirmation and calendar entry. Ask the owner if every remaining choice would require a fee, a different window or moving another commitment. |
| **Pay this account’s electricity bills up to ₹900.** The current unpaid invoice changes from ₹850 to ₹1,180 before any payment. | Compare the new invoice with the standing limit. Hold payment and put the original amount, new amount, payee and bill reference together. | **Pay ₹1,180 once** or **Leave unpaid**. The exception belongs to this invoice revision. If approved, wait for the biller’s matching settled receipt. The ₹900 limit still governs the next bill. |

The brief has one question. A person who opens it between meetings can decide the bill and leave. Underneath, they can find “Broadband booked for Tuesday at 3 pm” and “Watching for your ₹1,249 credit.” An approval, a provider booking and a posted credit each say something different. Every line should open to the record that justifies its wording. [Proposed desk experience](experience.md) · [Authority and evidence contracts](architecture.md)

## Give it something new

The next household will have different errands. Let someone hand over an unfamiliar outcome in their own words, such as “Track whether the library closed my old membership.” Agree on the membership reference, the issuing library, the allowed reads and the record that counts as closure. An email acknowledging the request keeps the file open. A matching closure record from the designated issuer can finish it. If the desk cannot access that source, it should say what proof is missing. The tracking instruction authorizes no cancellation or new contact.

The scholarship specialist applies the same principle to an already-filed NMMSS application. Official notices can move its school and district deadlines. A school’s acknowledgement of a student correction proves receipt. School verification hands the case to the district. The case finishes on the district’s own verification record. Public notice dates and simulated case events have separate sources. [Historical scholarship evidence](scholarship-sources.md)

## What we know and what we need to learn

A published survey of 2,243 UK adults documented time and well-being costs across ten areas of everyday administration, including bills and goods and services. This supports investigating recurring household work. Demand among Indian households, willingness to grant access, and time saved by Relay Desk remain open questions. [Everyday administrative burdens and inequality](https://doi.org/10.1111/puar.13709)

Recruit five adults who already organize household administration. Before showing the concept, ask each to reconstruct one recent follow-up. Record what triggered a check, who had to act, and the evidence that let them stop. Use synthetic jobs for the walkthrough and keep private records with the participant.

Then give participants the Monday brief. Without coaching, ask which item requires them, whether the refund has arrived, and what approving ₹1,180 changes for next month. Ask them to inspect the appointment and say whether they would have wanted the desk to book it. A participant who thinks the money arrived or the spending cap rose has found a trust failure. Revise the wording and repeat that exercise. Record unwanted interruptions, requested interventions and refusals to delegate separately from technical trial scores. Before any real pilot, identify an authorized route to the relevant issuer’s records and a way for the household to withdraw permission. No participant results have been collected.
