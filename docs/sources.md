# Source evidence

Deadline Relay follows the verification handoffs after a student files a scholarship application. The prototype reads public policy and scheme dates. Its case, student, institute and district events are synthetic. The evidence below establishes the public workflow and informs the test cases.

## Why the handoffs matter

The Ministry of Education tells applicants who have already submitted NMMSS forms to ensure verification by both the Institute Nodal Officer (L1) and District Nodal Officer (L2). The roles have different deadlines. The 31 October 2026 release reported 90,483 fresh and 199,531 renewal applications submitted by 30 October. These figures describe submission volume at that point in the cycle. [Ministry of Education, 31 October release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2330936&lang=1&reg=1)

In Report No. 36 of 2025, §2.13.4.2, the Comptroller and Auditor General of India observed ₹33.29 crore in undisbursed pre-matric scholarships to 2.52 lakh students in 11 states and union territories, and ₹54.79 crore in undisbursed post-matric scholarships to 1.06 lakh beneficiaries in nine. The audit identified NSP application verification delays among several contributing causes, including funding delays, bank transaction issues and difficulties with the payment systems. The figures cover the audit’s specific schemes and jurisdictions, and the report does not isolate the share due to verification. [CAG report, §2.13.4.2](https://cag.gov.in/uploads/download_audit_report/2025/Report-No.-36-of-2025_Civil_English_03-02-2026-signed-069ca36644bcd89.99772695.pdf)

The Himachal Pradesh Directorate of Higher Education describes the correction loop in its own November 2026 circular. It directs students to correct and resubmit applications marked defective, and institutions to reverify them. The circular covers specified central and state-sponsored schemes for the audiences it addresses. Its deadlines belong to that notice’s scope. [Himachal Pradesh circular](https://education.hp.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/centrally-state.pdf)

All dates here are for AY 2026–27. The date at the start of each item is the release date. The remaining dates are the three deadlines stated in that release. The replay reveals a notice only when the simulated clock reaches its publication. The historical press releases give date-only deadlines, so the evidence preserves calendar dates without assigning closing hours.

## Public NSP cross-check on 21 November 2026

The public scheme listing was available over anonymous HTTP and provided the following fields directly in its HTML. Capture the source URL and observation date with each parsed field so a later fetch remains distinguishable from a historical snapshot. NSP publishes eligibility information and public scheme timelines. Case status depends on the authenticated workflow or the simulation’s equivalent event. [NSP scheme listing](https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships) · [NSP student services](https://scholarships.gov.in/Students)

- **National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS).** Student applications closed on 15 November. The card showed defective application verification and institute verification through 30 November, and higher-level verification through 15 December. The 31 October Ministry release names the District Nodal Officer for that final step. [NSP scheme listing](https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships) · [31 October release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2330936&lang=1&reg=1)
- **PM-USP Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students (CSSS).** The card showed student applications through 30 November, defective application verification and institute verification through 15 December, and higher-level verification through 31 December. The dedicated announcement identifies **30 November for fresh applications**. The same announcement page also displays a separate CSSS and Jammu and Kashmir scheme renewal notice listing 31 October for student applications, 15 November for institute verification and 30 November for L2. The general scheme card does not specify a cohort in the observed copy. [NSP scheme listing](https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships) · [NSP announcements](https://scholarships.gov.in/ViewMoreAnnouncement)

Use the exact scheme, academic year, stage, audience, and geography when comparing dates. **30 November belongs to the NMMSS institute stage and to the CSSS fresh application stage.** When public evidence for a specific cohort remains unresolved, show the source records alongside the unresolved field and leave the affected calendar event awaiting review. The labels under `Defective Application Verification` name a portal milestone. The synthetic case supplies evidence of when the student is asked to correct and resubmit it.

## What the broad memorandum covers

Cabinet Secretariat DBT Mission memorandum D-12011/03/2026-DBT is dated **30 October 2026**. Its DoSEL and DoSJE pre-matric row gives 15 November for applications, 30 November for L1 and 15 December for L2. Its post-matric and top-class central-sector row gives 30 November, 15 December and 31 December respectively, with a Ministry of Labour and Employment exception. The two rows are separate scopes. Use the Ministry’s scheme-specific releases and NSP cards to map deadlines to individual schemes. [Scanned DBT memorandum, page 1, hosted by Saint Andre School](https://www.saintandreschoolbodhjungnagar.in/upload/2a3c4d61f532dc78126f39d1607199e2.pdf)

The Jalpaiguri district page dates its listing with the same subject **19 November 2026**. Its attachment did not load during verification, so the dates and scope above come from the signed 30 October copy. This is a case for keeping issuer date separate from repost date. Store the typed rules and their citations. The public scan itself contains personal details outside the project’s data scope. [Jalpaiguri district listing](https://jalpaiguri.gov.in/notice/extension-in-timelines-for-all-central-sector-scholarship-schemas-on-national-scholarship-portal-nsp-for-ay-2026-27-reg/)

## Existing services and implementation references

- **NSP and UMANG** already support application workflows and authenticated tracking for covered schemes. They provide the authoritative outcomes. Deadline Relay’s proposed role is following the student’s case between documented handoffs, including after a return for correction. [NSP student services](https://scholarships.gov.in/Students) · [UMANG service catalogue](https://web.umang.gov.in/landing/flipbook/index.html)
- **Buddy4Study** already offers scholarship discovery, updates and tracking on its platform. Its role sharpens the first product test. Can a source revision or returned application produce the right next step and evidence of handoff even when one downstream action remains pending? [Buddy4Study application and tracking guide](https://www.buddy4study.com/article/mastering-scholarship-applications-on-buddy4study)
- **Radicale and CalDAV** provide an independent local calendar against which to demonstrate an actual event update and read it back. Use isolated synthetic calendars without external attendees. Keep case-event simulation and message drafts local. [Radicale](https://radicale.org/) · [CalDAV specification](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc4791)
