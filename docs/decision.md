# Why Relay Desk

Deadline Relay established an evidence rule worth keeping. A school’s acknowledgement tells a family that a correction arrived. The school must issue a separate verification record before the case can advance. **Relay Desk** brings that distinction to everyday errands. Who can confirm this job is finished, and what help can the person get while they wait? The original scholarship proposal becomes one specialist example.

## What the previous proposal actually established

At `e7b729d`, the repository contained a scholarship proposal, dated public-source research, a visual study, a state-machine design and a test plan. Its historical probe used typed dates and in-memory records. Its result concerned two reminder keys. The repository did not contain a runnable planner, measured user outcomes, provider integrations or a submission recording. The audit below assesses that revision as a proposal. Inspect it with `git ls-tree -r --name-only e7b729d` and `git show e7b729d:docs/evaluation.md`. The older candidate comparison is preserved in `git show e7b729d:docs/decision.md`.

Homingo publishes five judging criteria for the edition. The A3 statement gives those criteria a particular audience, people who currently do their own personal administration. It asks for an assistant they could trust with that work. | Problem fit & impact | The Ministry notices established real deadlines and two offices with separate duties. One already-filed scholarship case gave us no evidence about other everyday follow-ups. | Technical depth | The proposed case had a fixed set of actors and event types. Changing a school’s words tested interpretation inside that one workflow. | Accept a desired outcome, scoped permission and an agreed source of completion evidence. | Reliability & evaluation | The proposal specified unusually good failure cases for stale records and duplicate writes. All release thresholds were targets. None tested spending authority or a stale appointment offer. | Keep the fault tests, add changed prices, revoked permission, competing bookings and a credit for the wrong order. Score actual provider records, forbidden effects and avoidable interruptions. |
| Product & experience | A clear single-case screen answered who had the next step. Someone would still have to inspect each file to find the decision that mattered. | Give a new user a clear next check on their first errand. As more errands accumulate, show the one decision a person needs to make and group work the desk can do with permission. |
| Demo & communication | The planned walkthrough established how to read receipts. The presenter supplied the correction and both verifications. It would have been easy to mistake the judge advancing the fixture for the agent doing work. | Start with a request made from a blank desk. Show the later, pre-authorized session separately. Let the judge alter an input or withhold proof, then see what the desk did and what the provider confirmed. |

## Why these three jobs belong together

A refund alone is enough to use the desk. Adding a broadband appointment and an electricity bill lets a judge test three different degrees of permission in the same session. Help a person follow up on money owed to them. Reserve a visit they have already permitted. Stop before spending beyond their instruction. An unresolved bill should leave the permitted booking free to proceed. A merchant’s promise should keep the refund open.

Use the library membership in the pitch to rehearse ordinary intake. The desk needs the membership reference, issuing library and record that would show the membership closed. Tracking a closure gives it no permission to cancel a membership. To test breadth, have someone outside the build choose a different goal and proof condition **after the rules are frozen**. Score whether the same desk can pursue it or explain exactly what access is missing. [Worked personal brief](problem.md) · [Held-out evaluation](evaluation.md)

The scholarship work still answers a harder specialist question. Public notices establish a deadline. Only the institute and then the district can verify a particular application. The four historical notices and the old probe belong to this example. They supply no evidence of refund settlement, demand for the broader service or time saved.
